President Trump is reinforcing his commitment to prevent Iran from acquiring a nuclear weapon, referencing threats against Iran's Pickaxe Mountain.

**President Trump**
- Is doubling down on his pledge that Iran will never obtain a nuclear weapon.
- Threatens to strike Iran's Pickaxe Mountain.
- Calls the conflict with the Islamic Republic of Iran a skirmish, noting that while negotiations occur, the regime remains unwilling to finalize agreements.

**Mark Wallace**
- CEO of United Against Nuclear Iran.
- States that Pickaxe Mountain is a deep-buried facility with a single military use: a nuclear facility.
- Notes that the president is correct in prioritizing the removal of the facility to eliminate ambiguity.
- References reliable reporting that Iran relocated centrifuges and enriched uranium from Fordow to Pickaxe Mountain before Midnight Hammer last year.
- Highlights divergence in whether the site is active or preserved for bombing, emphasizing the need to close or secure the facility to restrict Iranian access.
- Notes satellite imagery of suspicious activity around Pickaxe Mountain.
- Suggests that bunker-busting bombs and deep-penetrating missiles can neutralize the facility without requiring ground forces.
- Asserts that the current regime will not abandon nuclear ambitions, viewing the issue as leverage against the West, regional threats, and broader ideological goals.
- Describes Iran’s objectives as leveraging Western influence through proxy groups, threats to Americans and Westerners, and controlling strategic waterways like the Bab al-Mandab and Strait of Hormuz.
- Frames the nuclear threat as a concern for allies and a potential nuclear attack.
