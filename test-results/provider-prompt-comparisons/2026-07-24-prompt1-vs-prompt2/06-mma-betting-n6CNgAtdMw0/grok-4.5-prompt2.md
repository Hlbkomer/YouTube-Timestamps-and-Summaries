Hosts Cody and Paul of Bookie Beatdown break down the full UFC Abu Dhabi card, framing it as an ACA-heavy event with five undefeated fighters, five debuts, and plenty of violence and betting angles. They work fight by fight from the main event downward, weighing short-notice factors, styles, cardio, finishing rates, and price tags before closing with a shared “PRP” list of lean picks.

**Main event: Magomed Ankalaev vs. Bogdan Guskov**
- Ankalaev is a heavy favorite (around −550) after Khalil Rountree’s short-notice withdrawal; Guskov sits near +400.
- Both hosts like Ankalaev but refuse to lay the big money-line number. They note he rarely wrestles, often “plays with his food,” and has many close or subjective wins (including a draw with Jan Błachowicz).
- Guskov has power and early knockout wins but gasses, gets taken down, and has never shown he can handle a hard five-round fight—especially on short notice after fading against Błachowicz.
- Consensus: Ankalaev takes over in rounds 3–5. Paul and Cody both like “fight does not go to decision” (around −400) as a parlay piece; pure money-line is unappealing.

**Ramazan Timaev vs. Steve Erceg**
- Near pick’em / slight lean either way after Timaev’s year-long suspension for a banned heart medication.
- Cody likes Timaev’s power and has bet the KO prop (+225 to +250 range). He sees Erceg as hittable and not especially durable.
- Paul sides with Erceg’s experience, size, volume, and proven level (near-title shot vs. Pantoja). He worries Timaev is one-dimensional and may be affected by the medication ban or travel.
- Both treat it as a dangerous 50/50; Timaev must finish, Erceg likely needs a decision.

**Islam Dulatov vs. Wellington Turman**
- Massive favorite Dulatov (−1000). Turman is returning from a long shoulder injury and looked poor in prior UFC losses.
- Both view it as a promotional layup for the violent German prospect. They like Dulatov round-1 finish or under 1½ props rather than the bloated money line.
- Turman’s path (cage control, grappling with Glover/Pereira camp) is acknowledged but dismissed on tape.

**Magomed Zaynukov (“John Pork”) vs. Damian Zepki**
- Zaynukov −300 / Zepki +250. Paul takes the Dagestani volume striker and pressure fighter to win his debut.
- Cody strongly prefers Zepki (+250 and even +800 by decision), citing better movement, Topuria-camp training, youth (24 vs. 31), and a line that looks inflated by “OV” bias. He sees the fight closer to a pick’em on pure tape.
- Both expect a competitive striking match that could go the distance.

**Rizvan Kuniev vs. Tyrell Fortune**
- Kuniev −335. Both back the Russian heavyweight.
- Fortune (36) has suspect cardio, durability, and fading Bellator form; his only path is wrestling, which Kuniev stuffs at a high rate (93% TDD).
- Cody likes Kuniev by decision (+150) as a portfolio piece; late stoppage is also live if Fortune gasses.

**Said Nurmagomedov? / Saigid Izmagov vs. Abubakar Vagaev**
- Vagaev (recent ONE/ACA talent) is the pick near −265 despite a chin that has produced several clean knockouts.
- Both expect a decision or over 2½ if Vagaev stays disciplined. Izmagov looked gassed and ineffective against Nicholas Dalby; short notice hurts him further.
- Vagaev-by-decision and overs are the preferred angles once markets open.

**Valter Walker vs. Thomas Petersen**
- Walker (−175) has four straight heel-hook finishes and a gimmick, but Petersen is a strong wrestler with high TDD.
- Cody took a small Walker-by-KO flyer (+615), doubting the takedown. Paul still leans Walker by submission via scrambles and leg attacks, while warning Walker is injury-prone and one-dimensional.
- Live-betting angle: if Petersen survives the first few minutes, the fight swings his way.

**Dustin Jacoby vs. Muhammad Said**
- Jacoby −150. Both prefer the Glory kickboxer and proven UFC veteran over the short-notice debutant whose prior tape has largely been pulled.
- Said looks slow on available UAE Warriors footage; local-judge concerns exist but do not override Jacoby’s volume, cardio, and experience.

**Sam Patterson vs. Santiago Ponzinibbio**
- Patterson −400. Both like the longer, younger welterweight against a 39-year-old short-notice veteran coming off injuries and a long layoff.
- Chin concerns on Patterson are viewed as overblown at 170. Expect Patterson inside the distance or as a solid parlay piece; round-1 props are less certain.

**Ismael Bonfim vs. Axel Sola**
- Sola −185. Bonfim has explosive offense but fades, misses weight, and folds under adversity.
- Sola absorbs damage yet keeps coming; both expect him to take over after the first round. Cody also likes under 2½ (−120). Live bet on Sola after round 1 is highlighted.

**Magomed Tuchelov vs. Brenson Hero / Rivero**
- Huge favorite Tuchelov (−800 to −1000). Both agree he should win via striking accuracy and power (common-opponent KO of Kyle Morales/Machado), but the price offers no value.
- Hero is athletic and dangerous early but folds when hurt. Play Tuchelov sparingly if at all.

**Nurullo Aliev vs. Mike Davis**
- Aliev −230 and drifting. Paul takes Davis at plus money for superior striking and enough wrestling defense, while acknowledging Abu Dhabi “paint-fumes” history and control-time judging risk.
- Cody is waiting for a better price (hoping +200+); both like overs / fight-goes-distance props given low finishing rates.

**Abdul Hussein vs. Cody Gibson**
- Hussein −500. Both are sold on the Finnish-based prospect’s wrestling, pace, and regional résumé against a 38-year-old Gibson whose durability and cardio are clearly declining.
- Early unders and Hussein finishes are attractive; Gibson’s recent knockout loss and short-notice travel add risk.

**Closing PRP lean list and overall approach**
- Shared favorites/leans: Ankalaev, Erceg (Paul) / Timaev KO (Cody), Dulatov, Zaynukov (Paul watching Cody’s Zepki case), Kuniev, Vagaev, Walker, Jacoby, Patterson, Sola, Tuchelov, Davis, Hussein.
- They emphasize waiting for weigh-ins, limiting exposure on massive favorites and unproven “OV” debuts, and using parlays of mid-tier prices (e.g., Kuniev–Jacoby–Patterson). Early-morning Abu Dhabi start and potential local bias are noted as extra variables.
