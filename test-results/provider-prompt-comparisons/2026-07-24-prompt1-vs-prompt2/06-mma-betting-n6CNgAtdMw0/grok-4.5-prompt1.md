**UFC Abu Dhabi Betting Preview Summary**

Bookie Beatdown hosts Paul and Cody break down the UFC Abu Dhabi card, calling it a fun but under-the-radar event packed with ACA talent, short-notice replacements, prospects, and violence. They analyze matchups from a betting angle, highlighting line value (or lack thereof), fighter styles, cardio concerns, and preferred props or parlays while noting many heavy favorites offer little upside.

**Main Event: Magomed Ankalaev vs. Bogdan Guskov**
- Ankalaev is a heavy -550 favorite after Guskov stepped in short notice for Khalil Rountree.
- Ankalaev has a higher ceiling and should dominate later rounds with wrestling or volume, but he often “plays with his food” and exchanges on the feet.
- Guskov has power (dropped Jan Błachowicz) but poor depth, fades badly, and has never gone a hard five; his only path is an early KO.
- Both lean Ankalaev; fight likely doesn’t go the distance (strong parlay piece). Avoid laying the huge moneyline.

**Key Undercard Fights & Leanings**
- **Ramazan Taimirov vs. Steve Erceg**: Near pick’em. Taimirov has knockout power (post-suspension for heart meds) but low volume and limited depth. Erceg is bigger, more experienced, and volume-based. Cody leans Erceg; Paul likes Taimirov KO prop.
- **Islam Dulatov vs. Wellington Turman**: Dulatov massive -1000 favorite. Violent finisher getting a soft matchup against a faded, injured Turman. Strong lean Dulatov R1 finish/under; little value on the ML.
- **Muhammad Zankatov (“John Pork”) vs. Damian Zepki**: Zankatov -300. High-volume pressure striker favored to break Zepki down. Paul likes underdog Zepki (plus money + decision prop) based on tape and age; Cody sticks with Zankatov.
- **Rizvan Kuniev vs. Tyrell Fortune**: Kuniev -335. Superior striker with elite TDD against aging, cardio-poor Fortune. Lean Kuniev (decision prop appealing).
- **Said Nurmagomedov? Wait – Abubakar Vagaev vs. Saigid Ismagomedov**: Vagaev preferred despite chin concerns; better technician vs. short-notice opponent who faded vs. Dolby. Overs/decision props of interest.
- **Valter Walker vs. Thomas Petersen**: Walker -175. Heel-hook specialist (4 straight). Petersen has strong TDD/wrestling, so scramble/sub still likely but KO prop is a sneaky alternative. Live-bet spot.
- **Dustin Jacoby vs. Muhammad Said**: Jacoby -150. Clear skill/experience edge in a striker matchup; Said’s tape is limited/pulled and he’s on short notice. Lean Jacoby.
- **Sam Patterson vs. Santiago Ponzinibbio**: Patterson -400. Younger, longer, improving vs. 39-year-old short-notice veteran with fading durability. Strong lean Patterson (parlay piece).
- **Ismael Bonfim vs. Axel Sola**: Sola -185. Bonfim is a glass cannon who fades after early pressure and misses weight; Sola is durable/tough. Lean Sola (live after R1) or under 2.5.
- **Magomed Tukhugov? – Muhammad Tuchelov vs. Brenson Herero**: Tuchelov huge -800 favorite. Crisp striker and prospect vs. streaky glass-cannon. Agree with favorite but no value; play sparingly.
- **Nurullo Aliev vs. Mike Davis**: Aliev -230. Low-volume decision merchant. Davis has more tools but Abu Dhabi “paint-gate” history and age concerns. Soft lean Davis as dog or overs; many passing.
- **Abdul Hussein vs. Cody Gibson**: Hussein -500. Well-rounded prospect with amateur pedigree vs. 38-year-old fading veteran. Strong lean Hussein (possible early finish).

**Overall Betting Notes & PRP**
- Card is favorite-heavy with several -500 to -1000 layups (Dulatov, Tuchelov, Hussein, Ankalaev) best used sparingly or in props (R1 finishes, unders, decisions).
- Value spots: selected dogs/props (Zepki, Erceg, Davis, Taimirov KO, Walker angles) and parlays avoiding massive favorites.
- Concerns: short notice, travel/weight cuts, Middle East judging/control-time bias, unknown prospect ceilings, and cardio fades.
- Suggested PRP lean (Cody): Ankalaev, Erceg, Dulatov, Zankatov, Kuniev, Vagaev, Walker, Jacoby, Patterson, Sola, Tuchelov, Davis, Hussein. Wait for weigh-ins. Early-morning violence expected; focus on live betting and limiting exposure on chalk.
