The segment discusses President Trump’s renewed pledge to prevent Iran from obtaining a nuclear weapon, including the possibility of striking the deeply buried “Pickaxe Mountain” site. Guest Mark Wallace argues that the facility should be neutralized because it may support Iran’s nuclear program.

**Main Topic**
- Trump says Iran is being hit hard and may eventually seek a deal, but argues it is not yet prepared to negotiate reliably.
- He reiterates that the U.S. cannot allow Iran to acquire a nuclear weapon.

**Pickaxe Mountain Facility**
- Wallace describes Pickaxe Mountain as a mysterious, deeply buried site that he believes is likely intended for nuclear purposes.
- He says there are differing assessments over whether it is currently active or still being developed.
- Satellite imagery reportedly shows suspicious activity around the facility.

**Iran’s Nuclear Assets**
- Wallace cites reporting that Iran may have moved centrifuges and other technical nuclear equipment to Pickaxe Mountain before “Midnight Hammer.”
- It remains disputed whether those assets were being actively used or simply protected from potential bombing.

**Military Options**
- Wallace says the U.S. may be able to disable or seal off the site using bunker-busting weapons, without deploying ground troops.
- He argues that burying the facility and preventing Iranian access could effectively limit its use.

**Iran’s Intentions**
- Wallace believes the current Iranian regime will not voluntarily abandon its nuclear ambitions.
- He argues Iran uses its nuclear program, regional proxies, threats against shipping routes, and threats against Western countries and allies as leverage and intimidation.
