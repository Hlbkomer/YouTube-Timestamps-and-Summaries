**Trading Principles**
- Do not trade every market move or every asset; the speaker personally prefers focusing on only a limited number of setups.
- Avoid oversized positions and “full-port” trades.
- Take profits progressively during unusually strong or parabolic rallies.
- Match the trading timeframe to the instrument:
  - He strongly criticized buying far out-of-the-money calls based only on weekly-chart analysis.
  - For long-dated options, he recommends considering higher timeframes and understanding volatility and premium risk.
- The most important practice is to establish clear invalidation levels and acceptable downside before entering a position.

**Quantum Computing Risk to Bitcoin**
- He considers quantum computing a genuine long-term security risk but thinks current fears are overblown.
- His reasoning:
  - A quantum system capable of breaking Bitcoin cryptography could likely attack far larger targets, such as banks and financial systems.
  - Developers are aware of the issue and are expected to work on mitigation before it becomes urgent.
- He remains diversified and does not view the narrative as an immediate reason for panic.
