**Overview**

In this interview, OpenAI’s Greg discusses the company’s shift from conversational ChatGPT toward agentic AI and personal AGI. The vision is a nearly invisible interface where users state goals and the system uses tools, browsers, computers, and connectors to act on their behalf—unifying ChatGPT, Codex, and other products—while emphasizing trust, simplification, continued model scaling, compute scarcity, ecosystem partnerships, and transformative uses in health and work.

**Main Topic: From Chat to Agentic “Super App” / Personal AGI**
- Early ChatGPT was limited (no memory, tools, or context); the goal is an AI that proactively works toward user goals—organizing inboxes, booking appointments, solving hard or mundane problems.
- Ideal interface is almost none: persistent conversation with an entity that accomplishes tasks, like talking to a human assistant or coworker.
- Products are unifying (Codex coding agents + browser + ChatGPT) so prompts can trigger real actions via the user’s tools/computer.
- Long-term trajectory: less clicking/modes, more simplification and unification.

**How Agents Work in Practice**
- Models suggest next steps (e.g., health plan, travel agenda) then execute with consent—drafting/sending emails, gathering dietary prefs, building seating charts, using Gmail/calendar/Slack connectors.
- Codex already demonstrates this for non-technical users inside OpenAI (usage rivaling Slack); goal is bringing agent power to everyone.
- Early plugins (2023) failed due to weak models/short context; now models support hundreds of tools, large context, and high capability (unsolved math/physics, etc.).
- Trust is central and earned: users control delegation levels (draft only vs. auto-send), with oversight tools as a key differentiator.

**Ecosystem, Competition & Interface Future**
- Partners often welcome OpenAI making models better at their software, expecting higher usage; companies that block agents risk decline.
- Not framed as replacing OS/apps like iOS, but as a natural human-AI interface layer that brings machines closer to people (vs. files/folders).
- Competition (e.g., new Siri) is expected and healthy in the new agentic era; capability jumps let everyone rethink interfaces.
- Devices (OpenAI reported to be working on them) and better bidirectional voice matter for ergonomics and context, but the core is the always-available agent, not any single device. Current voice still has unnatural turn-taking; fluid, interruptible conversation is the target.
- Context (dynamic, meeting access, personal/business data) + trust boundaries are essential for high-intelligence agents to be useful.

**Model Progress, Differentiation & Economics**
- Scaling laws continue; no wall in sight after decades of skepticism. Progress requires hard engineering (custom networks, full-stack debugging) but remains achievable.
- Differentiation beyond raw intelligence: domain expertise, depth in science/industries, product/enterprise features (spend controls, observability, ROI tools), and vertical integration.
- Compute is the scarce resource in a compute-powered economy; demand will far exceed supply. OpenAI’s stance has been to secure as much as possible (including custom chips).
- Prices for a given intelligence level fall over time while frontier stays premium; history shows intelligence up + price down. Market is early and growing steeply; focus is meeting demand and delivering real customer value.
- Models are not pure commodities; value exists across the stack (compute, models, domain systems). Collaboration across the ecosystem is needed. Competition (including with Microsoft) is secondary to broadly deploying AI to transform the economy.

**AI in Health**
- Anecdotes (cancer diagnostics via ChatGPT/Codex, custom mRNA for a dog via AlphaFold + chatbots) are early signals of what will become standard.
- ~230M weekly ChatGPT health queries already; patients gain empowerment with data + models for second opinions, chart analysis, and rare diagnoses.
- Potential to accelerate personalized medicine, drug discovery, prevention, and reduce system burden/burnout. Deeply motivating for OpenAI leadership.

**Closing Outlook**
- The agentic era is here and will reshape work, science, personal life, and health. Progress continues rapidly; the opportunity is vast if trust, context, and wise deployment keep pace.
