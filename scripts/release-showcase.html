A visual route to higher dimensions

The video builds intuition for a fourth spatial dimension by first imagining a flat, two-dimensional world whose inhabitants can perceive only slices of three-dimensional objects.

Key ideas

  • A higher-dimensional visitor would appear as a changing sequence of lower-dimensional cross-sections.
  • Viewing a world from outside its dimensions reveals interiors and connections hidden from its inhabitants.
  • The same reasoning can be extended from three dimensions to four, even when direct visualization is impossible.
  • The analogy explains perspective, not every physical property of a fourth dimension.
